Privacy Policy — Postagem Meus Canais

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Home

1. Who we are

Postagem Meus Canais ("the app") is a private tool used by one independent content creator to schedule videos on his own YouTube channels. Contact: larderapaolo@gmail.com.

2. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What data the app accesses

When a channel account authorizes the app (scope youtube.force-ssl), the app accesses only that channel's own data that it needs to work:

  • the channel's ID and name;
  • the channel's own videos and playlists (titles, IDs, privacy status, scheduled date), to avoid duplicates and to put each video in the right playlist;
  • the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google.

The app does not access data of viewers, subscribers or any other user, and does not access Gmail, contacts or any other Google service.

4. How the data is used

Only to upload the owner's own videos as private scheduled videos, set their title, description, tags and thumbnail, and add them to playlists of the same channel. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

5. Storage and sharing

Tokens and a local log of what was scheduled (video ID, title, date) are stored only on the owner's personal computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to any third party or server other than Google's own APIs.

6. Retention and deletion

The local log is kept while the channels use the app. Any account can remove the app's access at any time at Google Account → Third-party access (security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). On request to the contact above, the stored tokens and log for that channel are deleted within 7 days.

7. Cookies

The app is a desktop program and this website uses no cookies or trackers.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
