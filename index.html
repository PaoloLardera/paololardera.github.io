What this app does

Postagem Meus Canais is a small program that runs only on the owner's personal computer. The owner produces short history and animation videos for his own YouTube channels (História dos Duelos, Canal da Bíblia, Tretômetro and Anime na Lata). The app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload those videos to the owner's own channels as private, scheduled videos, with the title, description, tags and thumbnail he already wrote;
  • add each video to the right playlist of the same channel;
  • list the channel's own videos and playlists, so the same video is never uploaded twice.

The app does not read, collect or publish data from any other user. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. The only accounts that use it are the owner's own channel accounts.
